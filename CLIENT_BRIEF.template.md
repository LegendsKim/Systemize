## Useful prompts if you want structure

- Who is the client and what does the business do?
- What problem should the project solve?
- Who will use it?
- What should a user be able to accomplish?
- Which pages, screens, forms, dashboards, or workflows were requested?
- What is essential for the first release, and what can wait?
- Which languages, countries, currencies, and timezones are relevant?
- Is login required? Which user roles and permissions exist?
- What information will be collected or stored?
- Which systems or providers must be connected?
- Are there reference websites, brand files, designs, or existing systems?
- What are the deadline, budget, constraints, and success criteria?
- What did the client explicitly say must not be included?
